MQTT Topics

Robots publish on:

robots/<name>/status
robots/<name>/radar

The web app subscribes to robots/+/* automatically.

Camera Streams

Android robots upload JPEG frames to the Node.js server:

POST /cam/<name>          (JPEG body)

Live stream shown in the web app:

/cam/<name>/mjpeg

Robot Radar

Each mini robot carries an Arduino + HLK-LD2415H radar over OTG USB. Detections are published on robots/<name>/radar and drawn on the Home map with the robot's own range rings (default 200 m). Click a robot row to change range, rings, color, GPS usage and heading.

Registration

Robots are added automatically as soon as they publish; you can also pre-register one with + Add Robot. Registered robots survive a page reload (saved in the browser).
